# Consent script — T-M1-003

Read aloud before the first question. Continue only after a clear "yes". Record "consent given: yes" and the date in the notes; do not record a signature or name.

## English
"Thank you for your time. We are designing a mobile app that would suggest du'a and adhkar at moments of stress, based on how a person uses their phone. We want to learn about your daily routine, your phone habits and your thoughts on such an app.

- This takes about 40 minutes.
- Taking part is voluntary. You can skip any question or stop at any time, without giving a reason.
- We will not write down your name or contact details. Notes use a code like P-01.
- We will not record audio unless you agree separately.
- This is not a medical or counselling session, and we will not give medical or religious advice.
- Notes are used only to design the app and are kept in our private project files.

Do you have any questions? Are you happy to take part?"

(If audio recording is wanted:) "May we record audio only to help write our notes? It will be deleted once the notes are written."
