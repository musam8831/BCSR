## Sampling (≥ 8 people)
| Slot | Matches | Target |
|---|---|---|
| A | P1: student, 18–25, heavy social media | 2 |
| B | P2: working professional, notification-heavy job | 2 |
| C | P3: 45+, prefers Urdu, moderate phone use | 2 |
| D | P4: 18–22, new to practising | 1 |
| E | Anyone who does not fit P1–P4 (to find a missing persona) | 1+ |

Mix men and women, cities and towns, and at least 3 people who prefer Urdu. **Adults (18+) only.** Do not recruit someone because they have a mental-health condition: that is the anti-persona.

## Rules
- **Anonymised only:** no names, phone numbers, employers or exact locations in any file. Use the participant ID (P-01…) and age band.
- **No audio recording** unless the participant agrees separately; if recorded, the audio stays off Git and is deleted after the notes are written.
- **Distress:** if a participant becomes distressed or mentions self-harm, stop the interview, check they are safe, and give a local helpline. The verified helpline directory is T-M1-011 (not ready yet), so before the first interview the interviewer must check a current Pakistani helpline number from an official source and write it on their own copy of the guide.
- **No diagnosis or advice:** the interviewer does not assess mental health or give religious rulings.
- Notes are written the same day and filed here; the tracker below is updated.
