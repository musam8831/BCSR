# Personas — REQ-02 v0.1 (to be validated with interviews in T-M1-003)

## P1 — Ayesha, 24, university student (Lahore)
Heavy social-media and short-video use, exam stress, late-night scrolling. Prays but finds dhikr hard to keep up. **Needs:** gentle nudges at the right time, sleep adhkar, du'a for worry. **Concern:** "I don't want an app judging me."

## P2 — Bilal, 34, software engineer (Islamabad)
Long work sessions, notification overload, irritability by evening. **Needs:** short adhkar between tasks, du'a for patience/anger, minimal interruptions. **Concern:** battery and privacy.

## P3 — Rukhsana, 52, homemaker / parent
Moderate phone use, lonely in the afternoons, prefers Urdu and audio. **Needs:** large text, Urdu translation, audio recitation, simple check-ins. **Concern:** complexity.

## P4 — Hamza, 19, new to practising
Little knowledge of du'a; wants to learn with meaning. **Needs:** transliteration, explanation of context (when the Prophet ﷺ said it), progressive learning.

## Anti-persona
A user seeking diagnosis or treatment for a mental illness. The app redirects this user to professional help (SR-01, SR-02).
